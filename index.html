A brief timeline

History of India

A short introduction to major periods. Each period contains many different regions, communities and events.

Ancient period

Early societies and kingdoms

The Indus Valley Civilisation developed in parts of the north-western Indian subcontinent. Later periods saw Vedic traditions, large kingdoms, new religious and philosophical movements, and empires such as the Mauryas and Guptas.

Medieval period

Regional kingdoms and empires

Many regional kingdoms and powerful dynasties shaped the subcontinent. The Delhi Sultanates, the Mughal Empire and several southern and regional powers influenced art, architecture, languages, trade and administration.

Modern period

Colonial rule and independence

European trading powers expanded their presence, and British rule spread across much of the subcontinent. A long freedom movement involved many people and approaches. India became independent on 15 August 1947.

Know the country

About India

Location

India is a country in South Asia, with the Indian Ocean to its south.

Government

India is a sovereign, socialist, secular, democratic republic with a parliamentary system.

Constitution

The Constitution came into effect on 26 January 1950, celebrated each year as Republic Day.

National flag

The national flag has saffron, white and green horizontal bands, with a navy-blue Ashoka Chakra at the centre.
