A village to be proud of

Rooted in the heart of Vidarbha

Arli is more than a name on a map. It is home: a place shaped by everyday kindness, familiar roads, shared celebrations and the connection between people and the land.

Set in Kelapur taluka of Yavatmal district, Maharashtra, Arli is part of the Vidarbha region. Like villages across this part of Maharashtra, its identity is connected to rural life, local traditions and the hard work of its people.

A note about the village record

Remembering our roots

Public village-directory records identify Arli in Kelapur, Yavatmal, with PIN code 445305. The 2011 Census-based village figures reported by public village directories list 1,818 residents and 429 households. These are historical census figures, not a current population count. Administrative details and services can change, so check official records for current information.
