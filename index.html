About India

India has a long and diverse history shaped by civilizations, kingdoms, empires, languages, religions, trade, science, art and social movements. Its history stretches from ancient settlements to the Republic of India today.

1. Ancient India

Indus Valley Civilization

One of the world's earliest urban civilizations, known for planned cities, drainage systems, craft production and long-distance trade.

Vedic Period

A major period of early Indian history associated with the Vedic texts and the development of important social, cultural and philosophical traditions.

Mahajanapadas

By around the 6th century BCE, several large states and republics developed across northern India. These are traditionally known as the sixteen Mahajanapadas.

Maurya Empire

The Maurya Empire became one of the largest empires in ancient India. Ashoka is especially remembered for promoting Buddhism and inscriptions across his empire.

Gupta Empire

The Gupta period is remembered for important achievements in mathematics, astronomy, literature, art and science.

2. Medieval India

Delhi Sultanate

A series of dynasties ruled large parts of northern India from Delhi between the 13th and 16th centuries.

Vijayanagara Empire

A major South Indian empire known for its political power, trade, architecture and cultural achievements.

Mughal Empire

The Mughal Empire ruled large parts of the Indian subcontinent and left a major legacy in administration, art, architecture and culture.

Maratha Empire

The Marathas became a major political power in the Indian subcontinent, especially during the 17th and 18th centuries.

3. Modern India

Arrival of Europeans

European maritime powers established trading connections and settlements in India. The British East India Company eventually became a major political power.

British Rule

After the 1857 uprising, governance of India was transferred from the East India Company to the British Crown in 1858.

Revolt of 1857

The uprising of 1857 was a major challenge to East India Company rule and became an important event in the history of British India.

Indian Freedom Movement

The independence movement involved many leaders, organizations and movements. Mass movements and political campaigns played an important role in the struggle for independence.

4. Independence and India After Independence

Independence — 15 August 1947

India became independent from British rule on 15 August 1947. The country also experienced Partition and the creation of Pakistan.

Constitution of India

The Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949 and came into effect on 26 January 1950. India became a republic.

Formation of States

India's internal boundaries have changed over time, including major reorganizations based partly on language and administrative needs.

India Today

Modern India is a democratic republic with a diverse population, many languages and cultures, and a large and developing economy.
